# Phase 025 (MOBILE-04) — Clipboard with phones
Status: BACKLOG — deferred by user decision 2026-11-23 (files first)

DEVPLAN reference: Phase 23. Depends on: Phase 021

## Goal
Text copied on a computer reaches the phone, and the phone sends its clipboard with one tap.

## Tasks
- [ ] Computer to phone: copies reach the phone's clipboard while the app is connected.
- [ ] Phone to computer: a button in the app (Android and iOS let no app read the clipboard in the background).

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Text copied on a Mac and a PC appears on the phone's clipboard while the app is open.
- [ ] The phone's button puts its clipboard on the chosen computer.
